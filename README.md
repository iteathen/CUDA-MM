# CUDA-MM

CUDA-MM is the reserved JavaScript/TypeScript owner for reusable, cross-domain physical memory-management policy in the CUDA-JS ecosystem.

CUDA-JS remains the sole native CUDA/provider boundary. Semantic consumers such as CUDA-JS-Tensor, CUDA-MCGS, cuda-data, cuda-io and products retain the meaning and logical lifetime of their resources. CUDA-MM may become the shared owner for domain-neutral allocation planning, suballocation, reuse, pooling, pressure, placement and related physical-memory policy only after consumer evidence justifies a production layer.

Repository creation does not authorize production implementation. See `AGENTS.md`, `docs/PROJECT_CHARTER.md`, the accepted architecture specification, and the activation issues once bootstrapping is complete.
